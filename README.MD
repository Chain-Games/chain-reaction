# 🎯 Chain Reaction

**Chain Reaction** is a fast-paced, competitive first-person shooter built for players who want real skill expression, tight gunplay, and high-stakes gameplay.

No gimmicks. No filler. Just load in and prove it.

---

## 🚀 Overview

Chain Reaction is designed as a **game-first experience**, with Web3 elements layered in to enhance progression, ownership, and rewards without getting in the way of gameplay.

Whether you're here to compete, improve, or dominate, every match is built around performance and decision-making.

---

## 🔥 Core Features

- 🎯 **Skill-Based FPS Gameplay**  
  Precision aiming, positioning, and decision-making matter every second.

- ⚔️ **Multiple Game Modes**  
  Including Team Deathmatch and evolving competitive modes.

- 💥 **Dynamic Combat Mechanics**  
  Weapon spread, recoil, overheating systems, and more.

- 🧠 **Real Skill Progression**  
  Improve over time through gameplay, not grind loops.

- 🪙 **On-Chain Integration (Coming)**  
  Optional Web3 features that enhance rewards and ownership.

- 🔁 **Continuous Weekly Updates**  
  Frequent releases with new features, fixes, and polish.

---

## 🗺️ Maps

- 🌑 **Moon Base**  
  Tight corridors, low-gravity feel, high-pressure engagements.

- 🏙️ **Slums**  
  Fast-paced urban combat with dynamic angles and close-quarters fights.

---

## 🧰 Weapons

- 🔫 **SMGs, Rifles, Pistols, HMGs**
- ⚡ **Photon Rifle** (charging + overheating mechanics)
- 🔪 **Rift Blade** melee weapon
- 🪓 **Throwable Hatchet**
- 💣 **Grenades**

Each weapon is designed with unique handling, recoil, and gameplay impact.

---

## 🎮 Gameplay Philosophy

Chain Reaction is built around a simple principle:

> **Game first. Everything else second.**

- No forced monetization loops  
- No pay-to-win mechanics  
- No unnecessary friction  

The goal is to create a game players would enjoy **even without Web3**.
